Upload a PDF EMC test report and get a summary of failures and out-of-limit emissions by radio band. Everything runs locally in your browser — the PDF is never uploaded anywhere.

Large reports (500–1000+ pages) are supported. Parsing happens page-by-page and may take a few minutes for very large files.

Failures are grouped by test (RE 310 / CE 420 / other), worst margin first, so you can see at a glance whether each individual test has a problem. The "JLR-EMC-CS" column cross-checks Radiated (RE 310) / Conducted (CE 420) rows against the exact limit lines in JLR-EMC-CS v1.0 Amendment 4 (Tables 7-1, 7-2, 8-2) — see the Standard tab for details and limitations.

Radio-service bands with at least one measurement over the limit (or, if toggled, within the marginal threshold), split by test — RE 310 (field strength, dBµV/m) and CE 420 (current, dBµA) aren't comparable measurements, so they're never combined into one "worst" figure even when they cover the same nominal band. Band edges are indicative reference data — see README for details.

Immunity tests (RF/BCI/ESD/EFT/Surge/transients, etc.) don't have a frequency/limit-line model like emissions — labs report them as Compliant/Deviation (or Pass/Fail/Not performed) instead, sometimes with a functional performance class. This tab surfaces those verdicts directly, including labs that use "Deviation"/"Deviated" in place of "Fail". Coverage note: category-level results (the dashboard below, and each subsection's overall "Test result:") are captured reliably; individual sub-test table rows within a large sweep table (e.g. every BCI frequency point) are not all itemized — check the source page for full detail on a failing category.

JLR-EMC-CS v1.0 Amendment 4 — RE 310 & CE 420 Limits

Reference limit lines used for the "JLR-EMC-CS" cross-check column on the Failures and All Results tabs, transcribed directly from the standard (Tables 7-1, 7-2 and 8-2). Frequency-dependent (log-formula) limits are shown as their formula; flat limits are shown as a single value. This is a cross-check aid, not a substitute for reading the actual standard — always verify against the source document for anything compliance-critical. Coverage is currently limited to RE 310 and CE 420; other tests (immunity, CE 410, RE 320, etc.) are not yet cross-checked against numeric limits.
